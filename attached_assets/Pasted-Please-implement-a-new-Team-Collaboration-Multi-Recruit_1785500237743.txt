Please implement a new **Team Collaboration (Multi-Recruiter)** feature to support collaborative hiring workflows.

## Objective

Enable multiple team members to work on the same job opening while maintaining clear communication, role-based permissions, and a complete activity history. The goal is to make the platform suitable for teams instead of only individual recruiters.

---

# Feature Name

**Team Collaboration (Multi-Recruiter)**

## Team Members

Allow recruiters to invite team members to collaborate on a job, such as:

* Recruiter
* Senior Recruiter
* Hiring Manager
* HR Manager
* Interviewer
* Admin

A single job should support multiple assigned team members.

---

## Role-Based Permissions

Each role should have configurable permissions.

Example:

### Admin

* Full access
* Manage team members
* Configure job settings
* View analytics
* Delete jobs

### Recruiter

* Review candidates
* Move pipeline stages
* Send assessments
* Schedule interviews
* Send offers
* Add comments and notes

### Hiring Manager

* View candidates
* Review resumes
* Add comments
* Leave interview feedback
* Approve or reject hiring decisions (if enabled)

Cannot edit job settings unless permission is granted.

### Interviewer

* View assigned candidates
* Submit interview feedback
* Add evaluation notes

Cannot move candidates or send offers.

---

## Candidate Comments

Allow team members to leave comments on candidate profiles.

Examples:

* Strong communication skills.
* Consider for Backend Team.
* Good culture fit.
* Follow up after assessment.

Support:

* Rich text
* Timestamps
* Author name
* Edit history

---

## Internal Notes

Add a private notes section that is visible only to internal team members.

Examples:

* Salary expectations
* Hiring concerns
* Internal recommendations
* Negotiation notes
* Reference check results

These notes must never be visible to candidates.

---

## @Mentions & Notifications

Support team mentions inside comments.

Example:

@Rahul Please review this candidate before tomorrow.

Mentioned users should receive an in-app notification and email notification (if enabled).

---

## Activity Timeline

Maintain a complete activity history showing actions such as:

* Candidate moved to Interview
* Assessment sent
* Interview scheduled
* Offer approved
* Comment added
* Internal note added
* Hiring Manager reviewed profile

Every activity should include:

* User
* Action
* Timestamp

---

## Candidate Assignment

Allow recruiters to assign candidates to specific team members.

Examples:

* Assigned to Rahul
* Assigned to Priya

Assigned users should receive notifications.

---

## Filters

Allow filtering candidates by:

* Assigned Recruiter
* Hiring Manager
* Interviewer
* Team Member

---

## Audit Log

Record every collaborative action, including:

* Comments
* Notes
* Assignments
* Permission changes
* Candidate stage changes
* Offer approvals
* Interview feedback

---

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Support multiple recruiters and team members on a single job.
2. Implement configurable role-based access control.
3. Allow comments and private internal notes on candidate profiles.
4. Support @mentions with notifications.
5. Maintain a complete activity timeline and audit log.
6. Allow candidate assignment to specific team members.
7. Provide filters based on assigned users and roles.
8. Ensure all collaboration features integrate seamlessly with the existing AI Agent, ATS workflow, assessments, interviews, and offer management modules.
9. Preserve security by ensuring internal comments and notes are never visible to candidates.

This feature will transform the platform from a single-user ATS into an enterprise-ready collaborative hiring platform, enabling recruiters, hiring managers, interviewers, and HR teams to work together efficiently throughout the entire hiring lifecycle.
