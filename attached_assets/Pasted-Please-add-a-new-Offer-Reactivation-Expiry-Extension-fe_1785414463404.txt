Please add a new **Offer Reactivation & Expiry Extension** feature to the AI Offer Management module.

## Objective

Allow recruiters to **reactivate or extend an expired offer** without creating a new offer letter from scratch.

Currently, once an offer expires, recruiters have to regenerate the offer, create a new PDF, and send it again. This is unnecessary and time-consuming when only the expiry date needs to be extended.

---

## Feature Requirements

### 1. Reactivate Expired Offer

If an offer has expired, display a **Reactivate Offer** button.

When clicked:

* The recruiter can set a new expiry date.
* The existing offer becomes **Active** again.
* No new offer letter should be generated.
* No new offer record should be created.

---

### 2. Extend Offer Expiry

For both active and expired offers, provide an **Extend Expiry** option.

The recruiter should be able to:

* Select a new expiry date.
* Save the updated expiry.
* Keep the original offer content unchanged.

Example:

* Previous Expiry: **20 July**
* New Expiry: **27 July**

Only the expiry date changes; everything else remains the same.

---

## Candidate Experience

If the recruiter chooses, the system should optionally send a notification email informing the candidate that the offer validity has been extended.

Example:

> Your offer has been extended. The new expiry date is **{{new_expiry_date}}**.

This email should be optional and controlled by the recruiter.

---

## Activity Feed / Audit Log

Record all actions, including:

* Offer Expiry Extended
* Offer Reactivated
* Previous Expiry Date
* New Expiry Date
* Recruiter who performed the action
* Timestamp

---

## Version History

The expiry date update should be recorded as a new version in the Offer Version History, while preserving the original offer document and previous versions.

---

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Recruiters can reactivate an expired offer without generating a new offer.
2. Recruiters can extend the expiry date of an existing offer.
3. The original offer content and PDF remain unchanged.
4. No duplicate offer records are created.
5. An optional notification email can be sent to the candidate.
6. All actions are logged in the Activity Feed, Audit Log, and Version History.
7. The feature integrates seamlessly with the existing AI Offer Management workflow.

This feature will save recruiter time, eliminate unnecessary duplicate offers, and make offer management more flexible while maintaining a complete audit trail.
