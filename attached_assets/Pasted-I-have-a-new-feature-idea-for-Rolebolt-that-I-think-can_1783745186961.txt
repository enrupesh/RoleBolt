I have a new feature idea for Rolebolt that I think can become one of our biggest differentiators.

Instead of a normal AI chat, I want to build an **AI Recruiter Copilot (Ask Rolebolt)**.

### How it should work

The chat must always understand the current context selected by the recruiter.

There should be three levels of context:

**1. Global Context**
No job selected.
The AI can answer organization-wide questions.

Examples:

* Which job has the highest number of applicants?
* Which role is hardest to fill?
* Show me all candidates with AWS experience.

---

**2. Job Context**
When the recruiter selects a specific job (e.g. Backend Developer), the AI should automatically limit its knowledge to that job only.

Examples:

* Who is the strongest candidate?
* Why is Rahul ranked #1?
* Which candidates have fintech experience?
* Generate interview questions for the top candidates.
* Who should I interview first?

The recruiter should not need to mention the job name every time. The selected job becomes the AI's context automatically.

---

**3. Candidate Context**
When a recruiter opens a specific candidate, the AI should automatically understand that all questions are about that candidate.

Examples:

* Summarize this candidate.
* What are the candidate's strengths and weaknesses?
* Why did they receive a score of 84?
* Should I hire this candidate?
* Generate interview questions based on this resume.
* What skills are missing compared to the JD?

Again, the recruiter shouldn't have to specify the candidate name. The selected candidate defines the context.

---

### Source/Evidence (Very Important)

Every AI response should include evidence.

If the AI says:
"Rahul has strong AWS experience."

There should be a clickable source such as:

* View Resume
* View Assessment
* View Interview Brief

or even direct links like:

* Resume → Page 2
* Assessment → Question 4
* Experience Section

The recruiter should always be able to verify why the AI gave a particular answer. This makes the AI explainable and trustworthy.

---

### Future Vision

The goal is to eliminate manual searching and filtering.

Instead of:
Search → Filter → Open Resume → Compare

The recruiter simply asks:

"Who is the best backend engineer?"
"Why is Rahul ranked above Aman?"
"Which candidates can join immediately?"
"Compare Rahul and Aman."

The AI understands the selected context, analyzes all available hiring data, provides an answer, and links back to the supporting evidence.

This should feel like talking to ChatGPT, but with complete awareness of the recruiter's hiring workflow.

This feature can become Rolebolt's signature capability:
**Don't search your ATS. Just ask Rolebolt.**
