Please implement a new **Shared Interview Feedback** feature to improve collaboration between interviewers before the AI generates its hiring recommendation.

## Feature Name

**Shared Interview Feedback**

## Objective

Allow interviewers and hiring team members to review each other's interview feedback after they have submitted their own feedback. This gives the team complete visibility into the evaluation process and ensures the AI generates its hiring recommendation using all available interview insights.

To reduce bias, interviewers **must not** see other interviewers' feedback until they have submitted their own.

---

## Business Rules

### Feedback Visibility

* Each interviewer submits their own interview feedback independently.
* Until their own feedback is submitted, they cannot view anyone else's feedback.
* After submission, they can view feedback from all other completed interviewers for the same candidate.

This prevents interviewers from being influenced before completing their own evaluation.

---

## Recruiter & Hiring Manager Access

Recruiters, Hiring Managers, HR Managers, and Admins should be able to view all interview feedback at any time, based on their role permissions.

---

## Feedback Summary

Create a dedicated **Interview Feedback** section that displays:

* Interviewer Name
* Interview Type
* Rating Scores
* Strengths
* Areas for Improvement
* Overall Recommendation
* Written Comments
* Submission Time

Clearly indicate whether feedback is:

* Submitted
* Pending

---

## AI Integration

The AI should generate the final hiring recommendation **only after all required interview feedback has been submitted**.

The AI should analyze all interview feedback together instead of relying on a single interviewer's evaluation.

---

## Notifications

When an interviewer submits feedback:

* Notify recruiters.
* Update the candidate's activity timeline.
* If all required feedback has been received, notify the recruiter that the AI Hiring Summary is ready to generate.

---

## Activity Feed

Record every collaboration event, including:

* Interview Feedback Submitted
* Feedback Updated
* Feedback Viewed
* All Required Feedback Completed
* AI Hiring Summary Generated

Include timestamps and the responsible user.

---

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Interviewers cannot view teammates' feedback until they submit their own.
2. After submission, interviewers can view all completed interview feedback for the candidate.
3. Recruiters and authorized team members always have access based on their permissions.
4. Display a consolidated Interview Feedback section with ratings, comments, and status.
5. Generate the AI Hiring Recommendation only after all required feedback is available.
6. Record all feedback-related actions in the Activity Feed and Audit Log.
7. Integrate seamlessly with the existing Team Collaboration and AI Hiring workflow.

This feature will improve collaboration, reduce interviewer bias, provide complete visibility into candidate evaluations, and ensure the AI makes recommendations using comprehensive interview data rather than isolated feedback.
