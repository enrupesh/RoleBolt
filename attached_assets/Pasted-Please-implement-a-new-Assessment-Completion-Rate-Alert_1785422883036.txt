Please implement a new **Assessment Completion Rate Alert** feature for the Assessment Analytics Dashboard.

## Objective

Proactively notify recruiters when the assessment completion rate falls below a recruiter-defined threshold, allowing them to identify potential issues early and take corrective action.

## Feature Requirements

### Recruiter Settings

Allow recruiters to configure a completion rate threshold for each job.

Example:

* Notify me if the assessment completion rate drops below **50%**.

The threshold should be fully configurable and can be enabled or disabled by the recruiter.

---

## Automatic Monitoring

The system should continuously monitor assessment completion rates.

Completion Rate Formula:

**Completed Assessments ÷ Assessments Sent × 100**

If the completion rate falls below the configured threshold, automatically trigger an alert.

---

## Email Notification

Send an email notification to the recruiter containing:

* Job Title
* Current Completion Rate
* Configured Threshold
* Total Assessments Sent
* Total Assessments Completed
* Report Generation Time

Example:

> **Assessment Completion Rate Alert**
>
> The assessment completion rate for **Senior Backend Engineer** has dropped to **42%**, which is below your configured threshold of **50%**.
>
> You may want to review the assessment experience, send reminder emails to candidates, or evaluate whether the assessment is too long or difficult.

---

## Dashboard Alerts

In addition to email notifications, display a warning banner or notification within the recruiter dashboard until the completion rate returns above the configured threshold.

---

## Activity Feed

Record every alert in the Activity Feed and Audit Log, including:

* Alert Triggered
* Current Completion Rate
* Configured Threshold
* Timestamp

---

## Alert Rules

* Prevent duplicate alerts from being sent repeatedly for the same condition.
* Send a new alert only if the completion rate improves and then falls below the threshold again, or if the recruiter changes the configured threshold.

---

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Recruiters can configure a completion rate threshold.
2. The system continuously monitors assessment completion rates.
3. Automatic email notifications are sent when the completion rate falls below the configured threshold.
4. Dashboard alerts are displayed alongside email notifications.
5. All alerts are recorded in the Activity Feed and Audit Log.
6. Duplicate or excessive alert emails are prevented.

This feature will help recruiters detect low candidate engagement early, improve assessment completion rates, and take timely action before hiring pipelines are impacted.
