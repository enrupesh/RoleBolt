Please implement a new **Interview Feedback → AI Synthesis** feature to enhance the hiring decision process.

## Objective

After interviews are completed, the platform should use AI to combine interview feedback with the candidate's existing evaluation data and generate an intelligent hiring recommendation for the recruiter.

The AI should assist in decision-making but **must never make the final hiring decision**. The recruiter or hiring manager must always remain in control.

---

# Feature Name

**Interview Feedback → AI Synthesis**

## Structured Interview Feedback

Instead of only free-text notes, each interviewer should complete a structured evaluation form.

The form should include configurable rating categories such as:

* Technical Skills
* Communication Skills
* Problem Solving
* Culture Fit
* Leadership (optional)
* Role-Specific Skills
* Overall Recommendation
* Additional Comments

Each category should support both a numeric rating and optional written feedback.

---

## AI Synthesis

Once all required interview feedback has been submitted, the AI should analyze and combine:

* Resume Score
* Resume Analysis
* Assessment Score
* Assessment Insights
* Interview Ratings
* Interviewer Comments
* Job Requirements
* Job Scoring Rubric
* Candidate's overall hiring journey

Using this information, the AI should generate a comprehensive hiring recommendation.

---

## AI Recommendation

The AI should provide one of the following recommendations:

* ✅ Hire
* ⏳ Hold
* ❌ Pass

Each recommendation should include:

* Executive Summary
* Candidate Strengths
* Candidate Weaknesses
* Risk Factors
* Key Reasons Behind the Recommendation
* Overall Hiring Fit
* Suggested Next Step

Example recommendations:

* Hire – Strong technical performance, excellent communication, and high alignment with job requirements.
* Hold – Good technical skills but concerns around communication; recommend one additional interview.
* Pass – Assessment and interview performance do not meet the required standards.

---

## Recruiter Decision

The AI recommendation should be advisory only.

Recruiters should always be able to:

* Accept the recommendation
* Ignore the recommendation
* Make a different hiring decision

The platform must never automatically hire or reject a candidate based on the AI recommendation.

---

## Recruiter UI

Create a dedicated **AI Hiring Summary** section showing:

* Resume Summary
* Assessment Summary
* Interview Summary
* AI Hiring Recommendation
* AI Explanation
* Candidate Strengths
* Candidate Weaknesses
* Overall Hiring Timeline

This should provide recruiters with a complete hiring overview in one place.

---

## Activity Feed

Record all events, including:

* Interview Feedback Submitted
* AI Hiring Summary Generated
* AI Recommendation Updated
* Recruiter Final Decision

Include timestamps and the responsible user for every action.

---

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Introduce structured interview feedback forms for interviewers.
2. Automatically generate an AI hiring recommendation after all required interview feedback is submitted.
3. Combine resume analysis, assessment results, interview feedback, and the job rubric into a single AI evaluation.
4. Display a clear recommendation: Hire, Hold, or Pass, along with detailed reasoning.
5. Keep the final hiring decision entirely under recruiter control.
6. Add a dedicated AI Hiring Summary section to the recruiter interface.
7. Record all related actions in the Activity Feed and Audit Log.
8. Integrate seamlessly with the existing AI Agent, ATS workflow, assessment engine, interview workflow, and offer management system.

This feature will transform the platform into an enterprise-grade AI Hiring Decision Support System by providing recruiters with a unified, data-driven summary of every candidate while ensuring that all final hiring decisions remain with human recruiters.
