This looks great. Let's move to the frontend.

I don't want a generic chatbot or a Claude clone. I want it to feel like a premium AI Hiring Copilot.

## Layout

A dedicated `/recruit/copilot` page.

Three-column layout:

**Left Sidebar**

* Job selector
* Conversation history
* New Chat button

**Center**

* Chat conversation
* Streaming markdown responses
* Recommendation card
* Confidence badge
* Sources
* Quick Actions

**Right Sidebar (Context Panel)**
This should always show the current hiring context.

Example:

Job
Backend Developer

Candidates
32

Average Score
74

Top Candidate
Rahul (94)

This panel should update automatically when the selected job changes.

---

## Welcome Screen

When no conversation exists, don't show an empty chat.

Instead show something like:

"Good morning 👋

I'm Rolebolt.

I can help you compare candidates, explain scores, generate interview questions and recommend who to interview next."

Below that, show clickable starter prompts.

Examples:

• Who should I interview first?
• Compare my top candidates
• Which candidates are missing Docker?
• Generate interview questions
• Show candidates ready to join immediately

## User Experience

The chat should stream naturally.

The input should stay fixed at the bottom.

Follow-up questions should feel instant.

The page should feel fast, clean and minimal.

---

## Design Language

Dark mode first.

Rounded cards.

Soft shadows.

Minimal borders.

No clutter.

No ATS-style tables inside the chat.

The chat experience should feel closer to ChatGPT or Claude, but every answer should clearly feel recruiter-focused.

The goal is that recruiters stop navigating through menus and simply ask Rolebolt.

This page should become the primary interface for interacting with hiring data.
