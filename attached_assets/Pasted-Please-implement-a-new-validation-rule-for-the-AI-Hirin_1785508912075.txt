Please implement a new validation rule for the **AI Hiring Recommendation** system.

## Feature Name

**Require Interview Feedback Before AI Hire Recommendation**

## Objective

The AI must **not** generate a **Hire** recommendation unless the required interview feedback has been submitted.

The purpose of this feature is to prevent the AI from making hiring recommendations based on incomplete information. Resume analysis and assessment results alone should never be sufficient for a final hiring recommendation.

---

## Business Rules

Before generating an AI hiring recommendation, verify that all required interview feedback has been completed.

If interview feedback is missing or incomplete:

* Do **not** generate a **Hire** recommendation.
* Do **not** generate the final AI Hiring Summary.
* Display a clear status indicating that interview feedback is still required.

Example status:

* **Interview Feedback Required**
* **Awaiting Interview Feedback**
* **AI Recommendation Pending**

---

## After Interview Feedback Is Submitted

Once all required interview feedback has been received:

* Generate the AI Hiring Summary.
* Analyze:

  * Resume Analysis
  * Assessment Results
  * Interview Ratings
  * Interviewer Comments
  * Job Requirements
  * Job Rubric
* Generate the AI recommendation:

  * Hire
  * Hold
  * Pass

---

## Recruiter UI

If feedback is missing, display a clear message such as:

> **AI Recommendation is unavailable because the required interview feedback has not yet been submitted.**

Optionally indicate which interviewers are still pending.

Example:

* Technical Interview – Pending
* Hiring Manager – Submitted
* HR Interview – Pending

---

## Acceptance Criteria

1. AI must never generate a **Hire** recommendation without the required interview feedback.
2. Resume and assessment data alone must not trigger a final hiring recommendation.
3. Display a clear "Interview Feedback Required" status until all required feedback is submitted.
4. Automatically generate the AI Hiring Summary only after all required interview feedback is available.
5. Integrate this validation into the existing AI Hiring Recommendation workflow without affecting current interview or assessment functionality.

This rule is intended to ensure that AI hiring recommendations are based on complete evaluation data, making the hiring process more reliable, transparent, and aligned with real-world recruitment practices.
