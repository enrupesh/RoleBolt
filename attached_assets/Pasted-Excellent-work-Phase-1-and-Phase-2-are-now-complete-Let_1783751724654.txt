Excellent work. Phase 1 and Phase 2 are now complete.

Let's move to **Phase 3: Global Context (Organization Intelligence).**

This is the final context level that completes the AI Hiring Copilot.

## Goal

When no specific job or candidate is selected, Rolebolt should automatically switch into **Global Context**.

The AI should understand the recruiter's entire hiring organization instead of a single job or candidate.

No manual filtering should be required.

---

## Organization Context

Provide the AI with organization-wide hiring data, including:

* All active jobs
* Hiring pipelines
* All candidates
* Candidate scores
* Hiring stages
* Assessments
* Interview status
* Talent pool
* Recruiters
* Hiring analytics
* Historical hiring data (if available)

The AI should reason across the entire organization.

---

## Example Questions

* Which job has the strongest candidates?
* Which role is hardest to hire for?
* Which job has the most applicants?
* Show me every AWS candidate across all jobs.
* Which candidates are interview-ready?
* Which jobs have low-quality applicants?
* Which skills are most commonly missing?
* Which recruiter has hired the fastest?
* Where are candidates dropping off?
* Which department should I focus on today?
* What should I prioritize this morning?

---

## Organization Insights

When the recruiter opens Global Context, don't wait for a question.

Automatically generate an AI Insights card.

Example:

Good morning 👋

Here's today's hiring overview:

• 17 new applicants

• 4 candidates are interview-ready

• Backend Developer has the strongest pipeline

• DevOps Engineer has the lowest applicant quality

• Docker is the most commonly missing skill

• Recommendation:
Schedule interviews for Rahul and Neha today.

---

## Talent Pool Intelligence

Allow natural language search across the entire talent pool.

Examples:

* Find candidates with React and Docker.
* Find everyone available within 30 days.
* Show candidates with fintech experience.
* Find senior backend engineers.
* Find candidates suitable for multiple jobs.

The AI should search across every job, not only one.

---

## Cross-Job Comparison

Support organization-level comparisons.

Examples:

* Compare Backend Developer vs DevOps hiring pipeline.
* Which role receives better applicants?
* Which hiring pipeline converts best?
* Which job should receive more attention?

---

## AI Principles

The AI should behave like a Head of Talent Acquisition.

It should:

* Analyze trends.
* Prioritize work.
* Recommend actions.
* Explain reasoning.
* Always provide evidence.
* Never guess when data is missing.

---

## Vision

Phase 3 completes the AI Hiring Copilot.

After this, recruiters should no longer need to manually search dashboards, reports, or candidate lists.

Instead, they simply ask Rolebolt.

The AI analyzes the organization, explains its reasoning, recommends the next action, and links every answer back to evidence.

This should become the primary interface for managing hiring across the entire organization.
