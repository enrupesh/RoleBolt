### 2. Better Markdown Rendering

Improve markdown rendering throughout the chat.

Support proper rendering for:

* Headings
* Bullet lists

* Tables

* Source type (Resume, Assessment, Interview Brief, etc.)

### 4. Recommendation Card

Whenever the AI provides a recommendation, render it inside a dedicated highlighted card instead of plain text.

Example:

Interview Rahul Sharma first because of the highest fit score and strong backend experience.

This should stand out visually from the rest of the response.

---

### 5. Streaming Cursor

While the AI is generating a response, show a blinking cursor at the end of the streamed text.

This makes the streaming experience feel much more natural and polished.

---

After clicking **Copy**, show a small success toast such as:
