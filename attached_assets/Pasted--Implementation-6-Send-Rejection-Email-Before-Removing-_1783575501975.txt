### Implementation 6: Send Rejection Email Before Removing an Applicant

There is another important improvement for both **Standard Jobs** and **Forum Jobs**.

Currently, each applicant card has:

* A **Reject** button.
* A **Remove (X)** button.

The current behavior is:

* **Reject**: Sends a rejection email and moves the applicant to the **Rejected** section.
* **Remove (X)**: Immediately removes the applicant from the panel without sending any email.

This creates a poor experience because the applicant never receives any communication and may continue waiting for a response.

### Required Changes

Update the **Remove (X)** button so that it follows a similar email flow before removing the applicant.

### New Workflow

* When the job creator clicks the **Remove (X)** button:

  * Open the rejection email composer (the same flow used by the Reject button).
  * Generate the rejection email using the existing AI-based email generation (do **not** change this behavior).
  * Allow the job creator to review and edit the email before sending it.
  * After the email is sent, remove the applicant completely from the panel (instead of moving them to the Rejected section).

### UI Improvement

Display a short highlighted note in the email dialog explaining why the email is important, for example:

> "This email lets the applicant know that they were not selected for the position, so they can continue applying for other opportunities instead of waiting for a response."

This provides clarity to the job creator and encourages proper communication with applicants.

### Apply This to Both Workflows

Implement this exact behavior in both:

* **Standard Jobs**
* **Forum Jobs**

The experience should remain consistent across both modules.

### Important Note

Currently, when a job creator clicks the **Reject** button in both Standard Jobs and Forum Jobs, the rejection email is already generated using AI. This behavior is correct and should remain unchanged.

The only change is to make the **Remove (X)** button use the same AI-generated rejection email flow before permanently removing the applicant from the panel.
