Please implement a new **Pipeline AI Recommendation** feature to provide recruiters with AI hiring insights directly on the candidate pipeline.

## Feature Name

**Pipeline AI Recommendation**

## Objective

Display the AI hiring recommendation directly on each candidate card in the Pipeline Board, allowing recruiters to quickly identify high-potential candidates without opening individual candidate profiles.

The recommendation should be a decision-support tool only. Final hiring decisions must always remain with the recruiter.

---

## Feature Requirements

### AI Recommendation Badge

When the AI Hiring Summary has been generated, display a recommendation badge on the candidate card.

Supported recommendations:

* 🤖 Hire
* ⏳ Hold
* ❌ Pass

Example:

**Rahul Sharma**

Interview Stage

🤖 **Hire**

or

**Priya Singh**

Interview Stage

⏳ **Hold**

or

**Amit Kumar**

Interview Stage

❌ **Pass**

---

## Hover / Quick Preview

When the recruiter hovers over or clicks the AI recommendation badge, display a quick summary including:

* AI Recommendation
* Overall Hiring Fit
* Top 3 Strengths
* Top 3 Concerns
* Overall Assessment Score
* Resume Score
* Interview Summary
* "View Full AI Summary" button

This allows recruiters to review key insights without leaving the pipeline.

---

## Real-Time Updates

The recommendation badge should update automatically whenever:

* Resume analysis changes
* Assessment results are updated
* Interview feedback is completed
* AI Hiring Summary is regenerated
* Recruiter requests a re-evaluation

Recruiters should not need to refresh the page to see updated recommendations.

---

## Status Before AI Recommendation

If the AI recommendation is not yet available, display an appropriate status instead of a recommendation.

Examples:

* ⏳ Awaiting Interview Feedback
* ⏳ AI Processing
* ⏳ Recommendation Pending

The AI must never display a **Hire** recommendation until all required interview feedback has been submitted.

---

## Filters

Allow recruiters to filter the pipeline by AI recommendation:

* Hire
* Hold
* Pass
* Recommendation Pending

This enables recruiters to prioritize candidates more efficiently.

---

## Activity Feed

Record all recommendation-related events, including:

* AI Recommendation Generated
* AI Recommendation Updated
* Recruiter Viewed AI Summary
* AI Re-evaluation Completed

Include timestamps and the responsible user where applicable.

---

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Display AI hiring recommendations directly on candidate cards in the Pipeline Board.
2. Support Hire, Hold, Pass, and Pending statuses.
3. Provide a quick AI summary without requiring recruiters to open candidate profiles.
4. Update recommendations automatically whenever candidate evaluation data changes.
5. Support pipeline filtering based on AI recommendations.
6. Prevent AI from displaying a Hire recommendation until all required interview feedback has been submitted.
7. Integrate seamlessly with the existing AI Hiring Summary, Team Collaboration, Assessment, and ATS workflow.

This feature will help recruiters scan the entire hiring pipeline at a glance, prioritize candidates faster, and make more informed hiring decisions while keeping the final decision under human control.
