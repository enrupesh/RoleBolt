Please add a new **"Review Zone Email"** feature to the AI Recruiter Agent.

### Feature Overview

Candidates whose AI score falls into the **Review Zone** (i.e., not shortlisted and not rejected) should receive an acknowledgment email informing them that their application is under review.

### Scoring Logic

* **Score ≥ Shortlist Threshold (default 75%)** → Shortlisted
* **Score < Reject Threshold (default 40%)** → Rejected
* **Score between Reject Threshold and Shortlist Threshold** → Review Zone

### New Agent Setting

Add a new recruiter-controlled setting:

* **Name:** `emailReviewZoneCandidates`
* **Label:** "Email review zone candidates"
* **Type:** Boolean (Toggle)
* **Default:** `OFF`

When this setting is **ON**, the system should automatically send a review acknowledgment email to candidates who enter the Review Zone.

### Email Behavior

* Send the email **only once** per application.
* Do **not** resend it if the candidate remains in the Review stage.
* Do **not** send it again if the candidate moves back into Review later.
* Log the email in the candidate's activity timeline and email history.

### Suggested Email

**Subject:** Your application is under review

**Body:**

> Hi {{candidate_name}},
>
> Thank you for applying for the **{{job_title}}** position.
>
> We have successfully received your application, and it is currently under review by our hiring team.
>
> If your profile is selected for the next stage, we will contact you with further details.
>
> Thank you for your interest in joining **{{company_name}}**.
>
> Best regards,
> {{company_name}}

### Acceptance Criteria

1. Add the new `emailReviewZoneCandidates` toggle to Agent Settings (default OFF).
2. Automatically send the review email when a candidate enters the Review Zone and the toggle is enabled.
3. Ensure each application receives this email at most once.
4. Record the action in the Activity Feed/Audit Log.
5. Support all existing email templates and localization mechanisms.
6. Do not change the existing shortlist or rejection email workflows.

This feature is intended to improve candidate experience by acknowledging applications that require manual recruiter review, reducing uncertainty without affecting the existing AI decision logic.
