Implementation 10: Complete UI/UX Redesign & Design System Polish

I want the entire application to have the same level of polish, consistency, and premium feel as the reference websites I shared. The goal is not to copy the design, but to match their design quality, visual hierarchy, spacing, colors, and overall user experience.

Currently, many parts of the application feel unfinished and unpolished. The UI/UX is inconsistent, and many elements blend into the background, making the interface feel flat and difficult to distinguish.

Overall Design Requirements
Redesign every page in the application with a consistent and premium design system.
No page should be skipped. Every screen should follow the same design language.
Improve the overall visual hierarchy, spacing, typography, colors, borders, shadows, and component styling.
Make the interface feel modern, clean, and professional while maintaining the existing functionality.
White Theme Should Be the Primary Theme

Currently, many pages are not optimized for the white theme.

Problems include:

Text is difficult to read in the white theme.
Some text almost disappears because of poor contrast.
In several places (for example, the Create Job page), the white theme looks incomplete and elements are not clearly visible.
Because of these issues, users are forced to switch to the dark theme.

This should be fixed.

The white theme must become the primary experience, and every page should look complete and perfectly readable without requiring the dark theme.

Please improve:

Card elevation

Borders
Typography hierarchy

Component consistency

The goal is for every element to stand out naturally while keeping the interface clean and minimal.
