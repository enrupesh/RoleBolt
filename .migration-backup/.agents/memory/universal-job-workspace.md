---
name: Universal seeker job workspace
description: Seeker workspace architecture for jobs sourced outside Rolebolt
---

The seeker product should treat a job workspace item as an independent job-search object: it can come from any public URL or pasted job description, is owned by the seeker, persists independently of recruiter-owned jobs, and can feed matching, resume, cover-letter, and interview workflows.

**Why:** Seekers should receive value even when they apply on LinkedIn, Indeed, a company site, referral, or email rather than through Rolebolt.

**How to apply:** Keep external-job data and recruiter application data separate. Use the workspace as the shared context for future external application tracking, reminders, analytics, and preparation flows.
