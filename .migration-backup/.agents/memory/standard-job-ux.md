---
name: Standard Job UX direction
description: Durable product distinction between Standard Job and Form Job workflows
---

Standard Jobs should feel like a decision-oriented hiring command center: candidate pipeline, rubric signals, stage movement, attention items, and recruiter actions belong in one workspace. Form Jobs should remain lighter and intake-oriented, focused on collecting and reviewing responses rather than managing a full hiring funnel.

**Why:** The two workflows serve different mental models. Blending them makes Standard Jobs feel generic and makes Form Jobs feel unnecessarily heavy.

**How to apply:** When adding or restyling recruiter surfaces, preserve the Standard Job command-center hierarchy and keep Form Job UI visually lighter, simpler, and response-centric.
