# Rolebolt Job Copilot — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** August 2026

## Overview

Rolebolt Job Copilot ("the Extension") helps job seekers save job postings and receive AI-powered match insights. This policy describes what data the Extension accesses and how it is used.

## Data we collect

When you use the Extension:

- **Job page content** — title, company, location, and job description text extracted from pages you visit. Sent to Rolebolt servers only when you click Analyze or Save.
- **Page URL** — the job posting URL, used to identify and deduplicate saved jobs.
- **Authentication token** — your Rolebolt session token, stored locally in Chrome sync storage after you sign in on rolebolt.tech.

## Data we do NOT collect

- Browsing history beyond the active job page you interact with
- Email or calendar data (no Gmail/Outlook integration)
- Passwords or payment information
- Data from non-job pages (the Extension only activates on detected job postings)

## How data is used

- Job data is sent to Rolebolt's API to generate match scores and save jobs to your Job Workspace.
- Your session token authenticates API requests as you.
- Data is not sold to third parties.

## Data storage

- Auth token: Chrome `storage.sync` (encrypted by Google for synced profiles)
- Saved jobs and analysis: Rolebolt servers, tied to your account

## Third parties

- **Rolebolt API** (`rolebolt.tech`) — job analysis and storage
- **Google Chrome** — extension platform and optional sync storage

## Your choices

- Disconnect anytime via the extension popup → Disconnect
- Delete saved jobs in your Rolebolt Job Workspace
- Uninstall the Extension to remove local token storage
