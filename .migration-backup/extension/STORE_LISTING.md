## Detailed description

Rolebolt Job Copilot is your AI-powered companion for job hunting on any website.

**Save jobs from anywhere**
Browse LinkedIn, Indeed, Greenhouse, Lever, Workday, or any company career page. One click saves the job to your Rolebolt Job Workspace.

**Instant AI match analysis**
See how well a job fits your profile — match score, strengths, missing skills, and actionable suggestions — without leaving the page.

**Live Copilot panel**
A floating panel on job pages gives you match insights and quick actions: save, tailor resume, open workspace.

**Works with Rolebolt**
Rolebolt is a universal career platform — track applications from every source, build ATS resumes, parse recruiter emails, and prep for interviews.

**Privacy-first MVP**
No email inbox access. Connect using your Rolebolt web session. Job data is only sent when you explicitly analyze or save.

Requires a free Rolebolt account at https://www.rolebolt.tech/seeker
