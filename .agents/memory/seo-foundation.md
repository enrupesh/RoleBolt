name: SEO foundation
description: Durable SEO scope and indexing rules for the Rolebolt recruiting and job-search website
---

The public SEO strategy is intentionally centered on Rolebolt's recruiting and job-search product, not the older broad all-in-one AI keyword catalog.

**Why:** Search relevance is stronger when titles, descriptions, structured data, sitemap URLs, and landing-page copy describe the product users can actually find on this site. Unverified ratings and unrelated product claims should not be emitted as structured data.

**How to apply:** Keep `/`, `/seeker`, `/recruit/opportunities`, public job details, `/creator/:username`, `/seeker/:username`, `/recruit/preview`, `/recruit/pricing`, and legal pages indexable. Keep dashboards, auth, billing, tokenized forms/offers, applications, filter variants, and job-linked recruiter detail views noindex/disallowed. Submit `/sitemap.xml` to Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools after publishing.
